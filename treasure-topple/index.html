Support

Thanks for playing Treasure Topple. If something isn't working, or you have an idea, please open a ticket at our support page on GitHub. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened, and we'll reply there.

Frequently asked questions

I don't feel any vibration. Open Settings → Accessibility → Touch and turn on Vibration. Then check Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics, and make sure Vibration is on in the game's own settings.

How do I trigger a big collapse? Look for the coin with a golden key. Collect it with a bag of its colour and everything it holds up comes crashing down.

I'm stuck on a hard level. Use the Hint button, or the Shuffle and Super Magnet boosters. Each retry also brings a new layout.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

常见问题

感觉不到震动？请打开 设置 → 辅助功能 → 触控 → 振动，再检查 设置 → 声音与触感 → 系统触感，并确认游戏设置里的震动开关是开着的。

怎样触发大塌方？找到挂着金钥匙的那枚金币，用同色宝袋把它吸走，它撑着的整块就会轰然倒下。

卡关了怎么办？可以用提示按钮，或者洗牌、超级磁铁两个道具；每次重试也会换一套新布局。

进度会丢吗？进度保存在你的手机上，删除 App 会一并删除。
