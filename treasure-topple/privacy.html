English

Summary: Treasure Topple does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

What the app stores

Your game progress, stars, treasures and settings (music, sound, vibration, language) are saved only on your device, using the standard iOS storage for app settings. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No account, login or sign-up.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • The game works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example by adding ads), we will update this page and the App Store privacy label before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: please open a ticket on our support page.

中文

摘要：《宝藏崩落》不收集任何个人数据，不上传到任何服务器，也不分享或出售任何数据。

App 保存什么

游戏进度、星级、宝物和设置（音乐、音效、震动、语言）只用 iOS 标准的 App 设置存储保存在你的手机上，不会离开你的设备。删除 App 会一并删除这些数据。

App 不做什么

  • 没有账号、登录或注册。
  • 没有广告，也没有内购。
  • 不包含任何统计分析、崩溃上报或跟踪 SDK，不跨 App 或网站跟踪你。
  • 不访问通讯录、照片、位置、相机或麦克风。
  • 游戏完全离线运行，不发出任何网络请求。

儿童

本 App 适合所有年龄，不收集任何人的信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

变更

如果以后的版本改变了以上任何一点（例如加入广告），我们会在该版本发布前更新本页和 App Store 隐私标签。
