English

Summary: Treasure Topple does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

What the app stores

Your game progress, stars, treasures, daily-challenge and countdown-calendar records and settings (music, sound, vibration, language) are saved only on your device, using the standard iOS storage for app settings. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No account, login or sign-up.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone, and the app never reads your photo library.

Game Center (optional)

The game itself works offline. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best collapses and daily scores are sent to Apple's Game Center to show leaderboards and achievements. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy; we receive no personal data from it.

Collapse replays (on your device only)

To let you share a big collapse, the app keeps the last few seconds of its own game picture and sound in memory on your device. It never uses the camera or microphone and never records other apps. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it. You can turn this off in Settings → Collapse replay.

Saving to Photos (add-only)

If you choose Save Image or Save Video in the share sheet, the picture or video is added to your Photos. The app cannot see or read your photo library.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example by adding ads), we will update this page and the App Store privacy label before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: please open a ticket on our support page.

中文

摘要：《宝藏崩落》不收集任何个人数据，不上传到任何服务器，也不分享或出售任何数据。

App 保存什么

游戏进度、星级、宝物、每日挑战和倒数日历记录，以及设置（音乐、音效、震动、语言）只用 iOS 标准的 App 设置存储保存在你的手机上，不会离开你的设备。删除 App 会一并删除这些数据。

App 不做什么

  • 没有账号、登录或注册。
  • 没有广告，也没有内购。
  • 不包含任何统计分析、崩溃上报或跟踪 SDK，不跨 App 或网站跟踪你。
  • 不访问通讯录、位置、相机或麦克风，也从不读取你的相册。

Game Center（可选）

游戏本身可以离线运行。如果你登录了 Game Center，你的最大崩塌和每日成绩会发送到苹果的 Game Center，用于显示排行榜和成就。这由苹果按其隐私政策处理，我们不会从中获得任何个人数据。

崩塌回放（只在你的设备上）

为了方便你分享大崩塌，App 会在你设备的内存里保留最近几秒自己的游戏画面和声音。不会使用相机或麦克风，也不会录制其他 App。除非你主动分享，否则不会离开你的设备。可在「设置 → 崩塌回放」中关闭。

保存到照片（仅添加）

当你在分享面板里选择「存储图像」或「存储视频」时，图片或视频会添加到你的照片中。App 无法查看或读取你的相册。

儿童

本 App 适合所有年龄，不收集任何人的信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

变更

如果以后的版本改变了以上任何一点（例如加入广告），我们会在该版本发布前更新本页和 App Store 隐私标签。

联系我们

有关隐私的问题，请到 支持页提交反馈。
